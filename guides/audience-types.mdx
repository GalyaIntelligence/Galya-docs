---
title: Audience types
description: "Audience types are groups of people with similar taste. List them and read their top members with the Galya clusters API, or view them in the dashboard."
api: "GET /v1/clusters"
---

[Audience types](https://app.galya.io/audience-types) is the dashboard view. Circles on the map are taste regions. The audience dropdown recolors affinity for one group of people. The domain selector matches the domains on [Catalog](https://app.galya.io/catalog).

The API calls these clusters. This playground lists them from `https://dev.api.galya.io/v1/clusters`.

People get into these groups from the links you write, not from a label you upload. Open a person on [People](https://app.galya.io/people) to see their mix.

<RequestExample>

```bash
curl https://dev.api.galya.io/v1/clusters \
  -H "X-API-Key: $GALYA_API_KEY"
```

</RequestExample>

`GET /v1/clusters/{clusterId}` returns one group and its members. Member ids are entity ids you can [search](/guides/search) or [recommend](/guides/recommend) against.
