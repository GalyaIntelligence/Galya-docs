The dashboard is [app.galya.io](https://app.galya.io). Access comes with a workspace Galya sets up for you. Sign in and pick a workspace in the sidebar switcher. Everything you send through the API shows up there for that workspace.

| Page | What it shows | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Catalog](https://app.galya.io/catalog) | Your items and what the model read from them | [`POST /v1/item`](/guides/items), [connectors](/guides/connectors) |
| [People](https://app.galya.io/people) | Shoppers and their taste | [`POST /v1/user`](/guides/users) |
| [API keys](https://app.galya.io/api-keys) | The workspace publishable key and secret | — |
| [Settings](https://app.galya.io/settings) | Account keys and members | — |

See [Authentication](/authentication) for which key to use where.
