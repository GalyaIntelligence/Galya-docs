Galya is a predictive taste model. It is trained to predict preference dynamics — how what someone wants forms and shifts with each thing they see, like or skip — over the view of your content that you specify. You describe that view in plain language as a [schema](/guides/schemas); the model reads your catalog through it and predicts each shopper's taste in those same terms. It works universally, on any catalog and any schema, without training.

**It understands your catalog.** Send your items and Galya reads every image and description, answering the questions you care about — *What occasion is this for? How does it fit? Diver or dress watch?* Your catalog becomes clear, filterable attributes instead of a pile of SKUs.

**It makes preference legible.** Tell it what each shopper does, in plain words like `"liked"` or `"added to bag"`, and Galya turns that history into a taste profile anyone can read: *black, minimal, work-ready; divers over dress watches.*

**It acts on it in real time.** Rerank a collection page, answer a search with intent, or recommend from the whole catalog. Each result is ranked by how well it fits that shopper and comes back with the reasons why. Every interaction updates their taste in the same call, and the same taste works everywhere you reach them: your site, search, email, ads or an AI agent.

No embeddings to build, no model to train, no ranking rules to maintain. You specify the view; the predictive taste model does the rest.

The API is at `https://api.galya.io`. The interactive examples in these docs call it with the key you paste.

## Three resources

| Resource | What it is | Main call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Item](/guides/items) | One thing you sell or show: a product, a video, a look. Its text, image and your metadata. | `POST /v1/item` |
| [User](/guides/users) | One shopper: their interactions with items, their taste, and every ranking for them. | `POST /v1/user` |
| [Schema](/guides/schemas) | The questions the model answers about each item: a [default schema](/guides/schema-library) for your domain, your own questions, or both. | `GET /v1/schemas` |

`POST /v1/search`, `POST /v1/rerank` and `POST /v1/recommend` are shortcuts for the common ranking intents.

## How it works

1. **Items are read once.** When you upsert an item, the model reads its image and text and answers every question in the schema with a probability per option. That answer is the item's [readout](/concepts/taste-schemas). It is the index: there are no embeddings to manage.
2. **Interactions are plain language.** Record what a shopper did with an item as a tag — `"liked"`, `"added to bag"`, `"watched 12s"`. Galya weights them and keeps the raw text.
3. **Taste is computed at query time** from the readouts of the items a shopper interacted with, through whichever schema the call names. The same history works for any number of schemas.
4. **Every ranked item says why.** Results carry `reasons`: the readout fields and options that lifted the item for this shopper.
