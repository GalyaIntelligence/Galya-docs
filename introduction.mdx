Galya ranks a catalog for each shopper by taste. You send it two things — your **items** and what each **user** does with them — and ask it to rank, search or recommend. One model, `galya-natural-language-commerce`, does the reading and the ranking. There is nothing to train, tune or choose.

<Note>
  Galya sets up each workspace as part of a [pilot](https://www.galya.io/pilot/). There is no self-serve sign-up. To get access, email [hello@galya.io](mailto:hello@galya.io). Once your workspace is live, your keys are on the [API keys](https://app.galya.io/api-keys) page.
</Note>

The API is at `https://api.galya.io`. The interactive examples in these docs call it with the key you paste.

## Three resources

| Resource | What it is | Main call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Item](/guides/items) | One thing you sell or show: a product, a video, a look. Its text, image and your metadata. | `POST /v1/item` |
| [User](/guides/users) | One shopper: their interactions with items, their taste, and every ranking for them. | `POST /v1/user` |
| [Schema](/guides/schemas) | The questions the model answers about each item: a [default schema](/guides/schema-library) for your domain, your own questions, or both. | `GET /v1/schemas` |

`POST /v1/search`, `POST /v1/rerank` and `POST /v1/recommend` are shortcuts for the common ranking intents.

## How it works

1. **Items are read once.** When you upsert an item, the model reads its image and text and answers every question in the schema with a probability per option. That answer is the item's [readout](/concepts/taste-schemas). It is the index: there are no embeddings to manage.
2. **Interactions are plain language.** Record what a shopper did with an item as a tag — `"liked"`, `"added to bag"`, `"watched 12s"`. Galya weights them and keeps the raw text.
3. **Taste is computed at query time** from the readouts of the items a shopper interacted with, through whichever schema the call names. The same history works for any number of schemas.
4. **Every ranked item says why.** Results carry `reasons`: the readout fields and options that lifted the item for this shopper.

## A first loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a key">
    Copy the workspace secret (`galya_sk_...`) from [API keys](https://app.galya.io/api-keys). See [Authentication](/authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a few items">
    `POST /v1/item` with a title and an image each. See the [quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a shopper's interactions and rank">
    `POST /v1/user` with `interactions` and `rank` in one call.
  </Step>
</Steps>
